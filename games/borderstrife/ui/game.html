Battle details

Battle reports appear after your first turn.

Movement

  • Shared land borders connect neighboring regions on new maps.
  • Select an army to see destinations and sea crossings.
  • Each order moves the entire army. Both sides move simultaneously.

Supply

  • Your capital and owned cities supply connected friendly regions, including sea-linked ports.
  • Isolated regions receive half their normal recruits.
  • Isolated armies attack at 75% strength and defend at 85%.

Combat

  • Crossing defense bonuses: rivers +20%, mountain passes +15%, sea landings +25%.
  • Terrain bonuses also apply.
  • Forecasts assume defenders stay put.
  • Defeated survivors retreat after all battles if a friendly route remains.
Orders are not saved. Keep this tab open and press Next Turn to save them to your campaign.
