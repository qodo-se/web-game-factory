Map sources

The 11 regional theaters use Natural Earth coastlines, administrative geography and river data. Internal borders combine or divide geographic areas into playable territories; they are not claims to exact political or historical borders.

Northeast Asia covers Japan and Korea, Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang, and the Pacific-facing Russian Far East. Inland Yakutia and Arctic Chukotka are outside this theater. Modern administrative source boundaries define the land coverage; they do not imply a position on disputed sovereignty.

Historical theaters

Five historical theaters use real modern coastlines and sectors centred on historical places. Internal lines are gameplay interpretations, not reconstructed political borders. Recommended ownership is balanced for play; it does not depict historical army deployments. The Greek–Persian and Viking maps are explicitly composites across multiple campaigns.

  • Crusader Levant: the Levant around 1187.
  • American Civil War: the Eastern Theater, 1861–1865.
  • Greece, Persia & Egypt: Greece, Anatolia, the Levant, Mesopotamia, the Iranian plateau and Egypt, 499–330 BC; composite. This selected theater does not cover the full Achaemenid Empire.
  • Viking World: Nordic homelands, Iceland, the Faroes, Britain, Ireland, northern Francia and the Baltic approaches to Ladoga and Novgorod, 793–1066; composite. Trade contacts and raiding destinations are not claims of Viking territorial control. Greenland, the Mediterranean and the route south to Kyiv are outside this theater.
  • Norman England: England in 1066. Modern England defines the outer theater; medieval frontier claims varied.

Research references: West Point campaign atlases, Library of Congress Civil War Maps, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, National Museum of Denmark, and English Heritage. Reference maps are not reproduced as artwork.

All maps use standard recruitment and conquest rules. Terrain symbols, vegetation and relief are illustrations. Land beyond the outlined map provides geographic context and cannot be controlled.

India preserves the approved Kashyap Meer outline and regional names. Afghanistan, Balochistan & Indus includes all Afghanistan, Iranian Balochistan, Pakistani border regions, Sindh and Punjab, while excluding Gilgit and Baltistan.
