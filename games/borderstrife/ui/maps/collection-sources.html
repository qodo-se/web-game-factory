Map settings & sources

Regional maps and historical campaigns use Natural Earth 1:10m coastlines and administrative pieces. Internal divisions are gameplay regions, not a claim to exact historical borders. Historical campaigns use a chosen period as their setting; they do not recreate every faction, alliance or opening territorial possession.

New battlefields and close-up city maps use measured modern elevation from Mapzen Terrain Tiles, with interpreted woodland, fields, routes and sectors. Current earthworks and development may survive in the terrain baseline. Illustrated roads, fort symbols and field patterns are not surveyed period detail. Starting strengths and deployments are authored interpretations designed for play. Troy uses an alternate Mapzen/Skadi elevation source to replace missing terrain tiles; missing elevations are never assumed to be sea.

Epics includes imaginative interpretations of literary traditions. Kurukshetra and Troy use modern terrain around their associated locations. Lanka uses the real island's coastline with invented epic place assignments. None is presented as archaeological proof. Emberfall has entirely original fictional geography, names and illustrated relief, with no measured elevation or real-world scale.

Cities, historical campaigns and epic maps use standard recruitment and conquest rules. The five new historical battles use existing fixed-force, 20-turn objective rules. There are no additional naval, siege-breach, supernatural, artillery or diplomacy mechanics.

Geographic data: Natural Earth (public domain). Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles and underlying source credits. Clipped elevation rasters, source tile URLs and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in the repository; play makes no external map requests.
